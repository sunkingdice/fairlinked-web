## Who is responsible?

**Fairlinked - Alliance for digital fairness e.V.** (the “controller”) processes personal data you provide when you contact us or apply for membership. Contact: [hello@fairlinked.eu](mailto:hello@fairlinked.eu).

## What we process

Depending on what you submit, this may include name, business name, email address, country, membership choices, and any figures you enter for voluntary contribution estimates.

## Why and on what basis

- **Membership applications:** to review your application, fulfil our contract or pre-contract steps with you, and meet legal obligations for an association (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where applicable, and (c) where law requires).
- **News and updates by email:** only if you give **separate consent** (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw that consent at any time.

## How long we keep data

We keep application data only as long as needed for the purposes above and as required by law (e.g. association and tax rules). Marketing consent is withdrawn when you unsubscribe or ask us to stop.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you may have the right to **access**, **rectification**, **erasure**, **restriction**, **portability**, and **objection**, and to **withdraw consent** where processing is consent-based. You may **lodge a complaint** with a supervisory authority.

## Changes

We may update this notice when our practices change. The current version is always on this page.
